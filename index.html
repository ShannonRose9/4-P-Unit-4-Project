Hidden History

Washinton Heights, uncommonly known, was a major spot during the American Revolution because of its view of the hudson river, and the boulder in Bennet Park is the highest point of Manhattan. If you walk around you will see many plaques and statues and even an old cannon



Parks

There are three major parks in Washington Heights

  • Bennet Park - the perfect childrens playground along with a soccer field and many many trees to climb
  • Javits Park - Another classic park, more new than Bennet, has swings, moneky bars, and a couple basketball courts
  • Fortryon Park - a beautiful park with many walking trails, loads of different event, many lawns to picnic in, and holds the cloysters
